These three images are rendered from public PTB-XL 1.0.3 ECG waveforms.
They are development preparation artifacts, not clinical photographs or annotated
training examples. No patient name or original clinical header is rendered.

Source: Wagner, P., Strodthoff, N., Bousseljot, R., Samek, W., Schaeffter, T.
(2022), PTB-XL 1.0.3. DOI: https://doi.org/10.13026/kfzx-aw45
Source license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.
Original publication: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-0495-6
Rendering code: ecg_validation_harness.py in this repository.
Selection/calibration/source hashes: image_preparation_manifest.json.

The rendered images have undergone a format change from native WFDB waveforms.
They are not expert-annotated, not licensed annotation targets, and not eligible
for training until source label semantics and image/time alignment are verified.
